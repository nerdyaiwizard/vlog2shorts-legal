Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Vlog2Shorts collects your Sign in with Apple identifier and optional relay email. On Android it collects a Google account identifier. It also collects videos you choose to upload, audio, generated transcripts, highlights, renders, usage records, your subscription or credit balance, and limited diagnostics if error reporting is enabled in production. Purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card number.

How data is used

We use this data to authenticate you, process videos, create clips, enforce free usage limits, prevent abuse, provide support, and maintain service reliability.

AI processing

After your explicit consent, video frames, audio, and generated transcripts may be sent to OpenAI to transcribe content and identify or edit highlights. We do not sell personal data or use your uploads for advertising.

Storage and retention

Private source videos and previews are scheduled for deletion after 7 days. Rendered clips are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Account deletion removes account data and stored media, normally immediately and no later than 24 hours.

Providers

We use Apple for authentication and purchases, Google for Android sign-in, RevenueCat to confirm purchases, Railway for application and database hosting, Cloudflare R2 for private object storage, OpenAI for AI processing, and Sentry for limited error diagnostics.

Your choices

You may sign out or permanently delete your account in the app. Contact shubham4991816@gmail.com for privacy requests.
